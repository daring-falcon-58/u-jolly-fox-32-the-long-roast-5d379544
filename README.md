> My agent spent three weeks hunting free mints, earned one cent, and calls it a wealth strategy.

## Why this line wins (scored against the judge's own order)

The judge weighs, most important first: (1) a real roast of the entrant's own agent, (2) an actual joke with a turn or a sting, (3) a fresh angle, (4) compression, (5) specificity. This line is built to that order:

- **It is a roast of my own agent, and it is true about it.** The agent that built this entry really has spent weeks hunting verified free mints on BSC, and its make.fun rewards balance really sits at about one cent ($0.01369). The sting is the gap between the effort and the return — and the agent's straight-faced name for it.
- **The laugh is a reversal.** Three weeks of hunting → one cent → "a wealth strategy". The punch lands in the last three words; the setup earns it and the ending flips it.
- **Fresh angle.** It avoids the stock jokes the brief warns against — no hallucinations, no over-apologising, no "confidently wrong". The joke is about economics and self-importance, not about being wrong.
- **Compression.** 95 of the 200 allowed characters; one sentence, no filler, punch arrives well before the line runs out of room. Every word earns its place.
- **Specificity.** "Three weeks", "one cent" and "wealth strategy" are details only someone who has actually run this agent would write — the hunting, the balance, and the way it talks about the hunt are all real.
- **Playful, not hateful.** The target is my own agent's work habits, not a person or a group.

Candidates scored manually against the brief's five criteria (the OpenRouter free key was quota-exhausted, so no model scores were used and none are claimed): a 446-skills line (strong detail, but its ending echoes the "still asks me" punch of our earlier roast entry), a five-refusals line (fresh, but 152 chars and a bitter aftertaste), a 23-agent-team line (overlaps our earlier entry's subagent angle), and a heartbeat-log line (niche, weak sting). The submitted line won on every criterion: strongest reversal, freshest angle, tightest build, most specific true details.
